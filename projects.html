Apex Tower —
A New Skyline Defined

New York City's newest supertall rises 62 floors and 278 meters above Midtown Manhattan. Apex Tower required solving extraordinary engineering challenges: a 40-meter deep bedrock foundation, a full-floor sky lobby at level 40, and a structural system capable of withstanding 120 mph winds without perceptible sway.

Our team of 400 engineers and specialists delivered the project 3 weeks ahead of schedule, under budget, with zero lost-time safety incidents — a first in AAYI's history at this scale.
