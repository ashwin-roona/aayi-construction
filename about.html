WHO WE ARE

16 years of precision, passion, and purpose in construction.

Our Foundation

Built on Trust,
Driven by Excellence

Founded in 2008 by a team of visionary engineers and architects, AAYI was built on a single conviction: that the built environment shapes human experience. Every structure we create is a testament to that belief.

Over 16 years, we've grown from a boutique construction firm into one of the most respected names in commercial, residential, and infrastructure development — delivering projects that define skylines and communities.

Our team of 850+ professionals combines deep engineering expertise with creative architectural vision, ensuring every project exceeds expectations on quality, timeline, and budget.

Our Journey

16 Years of
Building History

2008

Foundation

AAYI is founded in New York by David Harrison and Priya Nakamura. First project: a 12-story mixed-use development in Brooklyn delivering 240 residential units.

2011

First Landmark

Completion of the Meridian Tower — a 38-story commercial skyscraper that establishes AAYI as a major player in the NYC skyline and wins the AIA Award for structural innovation.

2014

International Expansion

AAYI opens offices in London and Dubai. First international project: a $300M waterfront development in Abu Dhabi, completed 6 weeks ahead of schedule.

2017

Infrastructure Division Launch

Dedicated infrastructure division opens. Wins landmark $800M contract for the Portland Metro Transit Hub expansion — AAYI's largest project to date.

2020

Sustainability Pledge

AAYI commits to net-zero operations by 2035. Launches green construction program that reduces material waste by 40% and earns LEED Platinum certification.

2024

500+ Projects Milestone

AAYI surpasses 500 completed projects across 38 countries. Apex Tower — our 62-story flagship — wins the Global Construction Excellence Award from the ICF.
